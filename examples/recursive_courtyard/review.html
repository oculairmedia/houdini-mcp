Judge the feeling

Does the transition feel like walking, shrinking, or falling inward? Try half speed, then the view behind you. Space pauses; arrow keys step through time.

Real Houdini geometry and OpenGL renders. A bounded moving window produces the continuing recursion; this is an architectural concept study, not a finished city integration.
